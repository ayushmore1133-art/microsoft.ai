Multi-Agent AI Interview Panel

4 AI experts.
One hiring decision.

Upload a resume and transcript. Four independent AI agents evaluate the candidate on their own, debate their disagreements out loud, and reach a weighted final verdict — with every claim backed by a real quote.

🧠

Isolated Agent Opinions

Each of 4 agents evaluates independently, with no access to the others' conclusions.

💬

Real Debate

Agents respond directly to each other's points — agreeing, disagreeing, or updating their stance.

⚖️

Weighted Decision

The final verdict is reasoned, not averaged — weighing evidence quality and confidence.

📄

Exportable Report

Strengths, concerns, and unresolved disagreements, ready to share with your hiring team.

How it works

← scroll or drag →
01

Upload

Drop a resume and transcript. PII can be auto-redacted before anything is sent to an agent.

02

Extract

One pass pulls out skills, claims, and experience into a shared, structured candidate profile.

03

4 Agents Evaluate

Technical, Culture, Hiring Manager, and Skeptic agents each judge independently — no shared context.

04

Debate

Agents see each other's opinions and must directly agree, disagree, or update their stance.

05

Weighted Decision

A judge step reasons over evidence quality and debate outcomes — never a simple average.
